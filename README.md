## What I build

I work across **machine learning, biomedical data, and Python software engineering**, with a focus on turning complex technical problems into systems that are understandable, reproducible, and testable.

- **Applied ML & computer vision:** object detection, multimodal learning, embedded inference, experiment evaluation.
- **Medical AI research:** physiological time series, uncertainty-aware prediction, calibration, and longitudinal modelling.
- **Software engineering:** Python, REST APIs, SQLite, data validation, automation, version control, and automated tests.
- **Reliability-first approach:** explicit requirements, rigorous data quality, reproducible experiments, and critical review of AI-generated code.

My background combines an **M.S. in Biomedical Engineering at National Cheng Kung University**, experience in academic research, and **approximately five years of enterprise data-quality work at Accenture**.
